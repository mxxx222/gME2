# 5 Strategic Prompts for Maximum 2026 Market Potential

> These 5 prompts target the highest-leverage opportunities for CoupleEdger to dominate the 2026 sexual wellness app market. Each addresses a specific competitive moat.

---

## Prompt 1: "The Trust-First Onboarding Flow" — Reduce Abandonment to <15%

> **Context**: 70% of users abandon intimacy apps during onboarding due to privacy fears, partner invitation friction, and unclear value proposition.
> 
> **Task**: Design a 3-step onboarding flow that achieves <15% drop-off while maintaining 18+ compliance and explicit consent.
> 
> **Requirements**:
> 1. **Step 1 (Value)**: Interactive preview — user experiences ONE card + timer before any data entry
> 2. **Step 2 (Trust)**: Privacy-first setup — local-first badge, data deletion demo, no-cloud guarantee
> 3. **Step 3 (Connection)**: Partner invitation — QR code + link + "try solo first" option
> 
> **Deliverables**:
> - Figma prototype with micro-interactions
> - Copy for each screen (A/B test variants)
> - Privacy dashboard mockup (shows exactly what's stored)
> - "Try solo" mode that's genuinely valuable, not a demo
> - Metrics: Time-to-first-session < 3 min, consent completion > 85%

**Success Metric**: Day-1 retention > 45% (industry avg: 25%)

## Prompt 2: "The Viral 'Couple Challenge' System" — Organic Growth Engine

> **Context**: Sexual wellness apps have near-zero viral coefficient because sharing is stigmatized. But couples LOVE sharing *achievements*, not activities.
> 
> **Task**: Design a "Couple Challenge" feature that generates organic sharing without exposing intimate details.
> 
> **Core Mechanic**: 
> - 7/14/30-day themed challenges (e.g., "Intimacy Reboot", "Communication Sprint", "Sensory Exploration")
> - Each day = 1 shared card + 1 private reflection
> - Completion unlocks a **shareable badge** (e.g., "We completed 14 days of intimacy")
> - Badge shows ONLY: challenge name, duration, completion date — ZERO session details
> 
> **Viral Loop**:
> 1. Couple completes challenge → earns badge
> 2. Badge has "Share with partner" → generates beautiful Instagram Story template
> 3. Template says "We just finished 14 days of [Challenge Name] 💕" + app deep link
> 4. Friends see → curiosity → download
> 
> **Deliverables**:
> - 12 themed challenges (seasonal + evergreen)
> - Badge design system (12 base + 4 seasonal variants)
> - Instagram Story / TikTok template generator
> - Referral tracking: "Invited by [Partner Name]" → both get bonus theme
> - Privacy: Zero session data in share payload

**Success Metric**: K-factor > 0.3 (1 in 3 couples invites another couple)

## Prompt 3: "The 'Therapist-Approved' Trust Badge" — Clinical Credibility Moat

> **Context**: 2026 users are skeptical of wellness apps. Clinical validation is the #1 trust signal — but no competitor has it.
> 
> **Task**: Design a "Clinical Advisory Board" integration that earns a legitimate "Therapist-Recommended" badge without making medical claims.
> 
> **Strategy**:
> 1. Recruit 3-5 certified sex therapists / relationship researchers as advisors (paid equity)
> 2. They review: card content, safety flows, consent architecture, aftercare design
> 3. Earn: "Reviewed by [Dr. Name], PhD, AASECT Certified Sex Therapist" badge
> 4. Display: On App Store, website, onboarding, settings
> 
> **Product Integration**:
> - "Therapist's Note" on select cards (e.g., "Dr. Smith: 'This pause technique mirrors sensate focus therapy'")
> - "Clinical Rationale" expandable on sensitive cards (prostate, CNC, sensory deprivation)
> - Quarterly "Ask a Therapist" AMA in-app (moderated, anonymous questions)
> - Annual clinical review → "2026 Review Complete" badge update
> 
> **Legal**: No medical claims. Badge = "Content reviewed by certified professionals" not "clinically proven"
> 
> **Deliverables**:
> - Advisor recruitment pitch deck
> - Badge design (trust signals: certifications, institutions)
> - In-app "Clinical Rationale" UI component
> - AMA scheduling + moderation workflow
> - Press kit: "First intimacy app with clinical advisory board"

**Success Metric**: App Store conversion +15%, press mentions > 20 in Year 1

## Prompt 4: "The 'Inclusive by Default' Accessibility Suite" — Untapped 40% Market

> **Context**: 40% of adults have some disability affecting sexual wellness. Zero competitors serve them well.
> 
> **Task**: Build the first truly accessible intimacy app — not "accessible compliant" but "designed for disability first."
> 
> **Disabilities to Serve** (Priority Order):
> 1. **Visual** (screen reader, high contrast, scalable text, haptic-only mode)
> 2. **Motor** (voice control, switch access, large tap targets, gesture alternatives)
> 3. **Cognitive** (simplified language, predictable patterns, no timed pressure)
> 4. **Chronic Pain/Fatigue** (auto-pause on fatigue signals, energy-aware session lengths)
> 4. **Hearing** (visual cues for all audio, haptic patterns for cues)
> 5. **Neurodivergent** (predictable patterns, sensory-friendly mode, explicit consent)
> 
> **Technical Requirements**:
> - WCAG 2.1 AAA (not AA)
> - VoiceOver / TalkBack: Every card, timer state, feedback option announced
> - Haptic-first: Entire session navigable via vibration patterns alone
> - Voice Control: "Start session", "Pause", "Next card", "Stop" via voice
> - Reduced Motion: Zero animations option (not just reduced)
> - Text Scaling: Up to 300% without horizontal scroll
> - Color Blind Safe: All status communicated via shape + text + pattern
> 
> **Content**: 
> - Disability-inclusive card language (tested with community)
> - Chronic pain positioning guides (illustrated)
> - Medication/condition interaction notes (e.g., "SSRIs may affect arousal timing")
> 
> **Deliverables**:
> - Accessibility audit checklist (WCAG 2.1 AAA + custom intimate-app criteria)
> - Screen reader test scripts for every flow
> - Haptic pattern library (20+ distinct patterns)
> - Voice command vocabulary
> - Disability community beta program (recruit 50+ testers)
> - "Accessibility First" marketing page

**Success Metric**: 25%+ users enable at least one accessibility feature; App Store "Accessibility" feature

## Prompt 5: "The Subscription That Pays for Itself" — Revenue + Retention Moat

> **Context**: Subscription fatigue is real. Users cancel if they don't feel daily value. But they KEEP subscriptions that save them money elsewhere.
> 
> **Task**: Design a "Pleasure Pass" subscription where the value exceeds the cost via partner ecosystem partnerships.
> 
> **Core Concept**: $9.99/mo or $79/yr includes:
> 1. **App Premium**: All modes, all cards, AI voice coach, unlimited routines
> 2. **Partner Discounts** (worth $200+/yr):
>    - 20% off Lovense, We-Vibe, Dame, Maude, Maude
>    - 15% off organic lube brands (Sustain, Good Clean Love)
>    - 10% off ethical porn/erotica (Dipsea, Quinn, Frolic)
>    - 20% off sex therapy directories (AASECT, Modern Intimacy)
>    - Free quarterly "Intimacy Kit" (sample lube, toy cleaner, guide)
> 3. **Insurance/Reimbursement**: HSA/FSA eligible receipt generation
> 4. **Relationship ROI Calculator**: "You saved $X on toys + therapy this year"
> 
> **Retention Mechanics**:
> - "Streak protection": Miss a month? No penalty, just pause
> - "Relationship anniversary": Free month on your couple anniversary
> - "Therapy fund": 5% of subscription → couple's therapy savings jar
> - "Gift a month": Send to friend (they get 1 month free, you get 1 free)
> 
> **Deliverables**:
> - Partnership outreach templates (50+ target brands)
> - Pricing page with value calculator ("Your savings: $247/yr")
> - HSA/FSA receipt generator (IRS-compliant)
> - Anniversary detection + gift flow
> - Cancellation flow: "Pause instead?" → keeps data, resumes free
> - Referral: "Give 50% off, get 50% off" (both ways)

**Success Metric**: LTV > $200, churn < 3% monthly, 40%+ annual plan uptake
